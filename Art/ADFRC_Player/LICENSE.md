## 1. Authorisation

The assets in this folder were extracted from the **ADF Re-Cut (ADFRC)** Arma 3 mod
pack by a member of the ADF Re-Cut team and supplied to the producer. The producer
confirms he has been given authorisation to use them in Southern Spear, and that
**"Tonnie" and "Brucey" are the same person** — Brucey being the author name that
appears throughout this pack's own configuration files. The producer further
confirms the Re-Cut team are content for these to be used in his free project.

The authorisation is evidenced by the email transcribed verbatim below. The
original screenshot is preserved at
`Docs/evidence/L0021_adfrc_authorisation_email.png`, and a full transcription at
`Docs/evidence/L0021_adfrc_authorisation_email.txt`.

## 3. What this authorises

Use of these assets **within the Southern Spear project**, for development,
testing, prototyping, and inclusion within the game, in their current unextracted
format. The producer's decision to accept these terms is recorded here so the
basis of use is unambiguous to anyone reading this folder later.

## 4. What the grantor expressly left with the recipient

The third paragraph of the email is a **disclaimer, not a warranty**. The grantor
grants permission on the understanding that **the recipient remains responsible for
compliance with any applicable intellectual property, licensing, copyright or other
legal requirements**. The grantor does not warrant that the material is free of
third-party rights. This is reproduced here because it governs how the assets may
be shipped.
