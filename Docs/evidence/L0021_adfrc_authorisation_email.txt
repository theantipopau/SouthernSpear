WHAT THIS DOCUMENT DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------------------------
ESTABLISHED
  * A written, dated, named grant exists, addressed to the producer and
    naming the Southern Spear project specifically.
  * The grant is expressed to cover the extracted assets "in that
    unextracted format", and to permit use for development, testing,
    prototyping, and inclusion within the game.
  * The grantor states he performed the extraction himself ("assets
    that I have extracted"), and supplied them as a ZIP archive.

EXPRESSLY RESERVED BACK TO THE RECIPIENT BY THE GRANTOR
  * The third paragraph is a disclaimer, not a warranty. The grantor
    grants permission "on the understanding that you remain responsible
    for ensuring compliance with any applicable intellectual property,
    licensing, copyright, or other legal requirements associated with
    the original source material." He therefore does NOT warrant that
    the material is free of third-party rights, and does not warrant
    that he holds all the rights he is granting. This is recorded
    because it is the single most important sentence in the message.

OPEN QUESTION THE GRANT DOES NOT ITSELF ANSWER
  * ADF Re-Cut is a multi-author pack. Author strings in the extracted
    configs name: Brucey, Exer, Growlor, Louetta, Quiggs, "ADFU Team",
    "ADF Re-Cut Team". "Tonnie" does not appear among them.
  * A grantor can only license what he owns. The register therefore
    records this as covering the grantor's own components, with
    per-component confirmation outstanding for any Brucey / Exer /
    Growlor / Louetta / Quiggs material that is actually imported.
  * APL-SA is imposed by Bohemia Interactive on the Arma distribution
    side and is not within a community author's gift to waive. It is
    non-commercial and Arma-only. This grant does not address it.

INDEPENDENT OF THIS GRANT
  * Third-party manufacturer identities present in the material
    (Crye Precision / G3, Ops-Core, PASGT, "Team Wendy") are the
    property of companies that are not party to this email.
  * Real ADF camouflage patterns and ADF service identities are the
    property of the Australian Defence Force / Department of Defence,
    also not party to this email, and are the subject of the project's
    standing prohibitions ADR-016 and L-0004 / L-0007.
  * These are recorded as open. They are not assertions of infringement;
    they are the items requiring legal review before release.
